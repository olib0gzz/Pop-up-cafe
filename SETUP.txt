COFFEE COUNTER POS - IPHONE SETUP

1. PUT THE FOLDER ONLINE ONCE (needed so the iPhone can install it)
   Upload all files in this folder, as they are, to any free HTTPS static host:
   - GitHub Pages: new public repo > upload these files > Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch "main".
   - Netlify: sign in > Add new site > Deploy manually > drag this folder in.
   You get a link like https://yourname.github.io/coffee-pos/

2. INSTALL ON THE IPHONE
   - AirPrint printer, or a printer whose app accepts shared images:
     Open the link in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen. Open it from the Home Screen once
     while online. After that it works with no internet.
     In the app: Settings > Printer > "Share sheet".
   - Cheap Bluetooth thermal printer (58/80 mm, ESC/POS, Bluetooth LE):
     Install the free "Bluefy - Web BLE Browser" app, open the link in Bluefy and bookmark it.
     In the app: Settings > Printer > "Bluetooth" > Connect printer > Test print.
     Note: Bluefy needs the page loaded once per session; keep its tab open during the event.

3. EVERY DAY
   Report tab > Print end-of-day report. Settings > Save backup.
   Sales live only on that phone until you back them up.

UPDATING
   If you change index.html later, also change VERSION in sw.js (e.g. coffee-pos-v2)
   so installed phones pick up the new copy.

INVENTORY (added Nov 2026)
   - Inventory tab > Count stock: enter what you have on hand once. From then on every sale
     takes its recipe ingredients out automatically, and a void puts them back.
   - Tap an ingredient to log "Bought more" (also updates its cost), a recount, or waste.
   - Menu tab > tap an item > its recipe button to change amounts. Cost and margin update live.
   - Report tab shows cost of goods, gross profit and ingredients used for any date range.

NIIMBOT B21S LABEL PRINTING (added Nov 2026)
   - Receipts are laid out for 50 x 80 mm labels (T50*80). Receipt and barista ticket print as
     two labels in one go; a long order continues onto another label.
   - Direct printing needs Bluetooth, which Safari does not have. On iPhone, open the app link in
     the free Bluefy browser, then Settings > Printer > Niimbot > Connect printer > Test print.
   - Close the official NIIMBOT app first: the printer only talks to one app at a time.
   - Niimbot printing uses NiimBlueLib by MultiMote (MIT licence, see niimbluelib.LICENSE.txt).
